# Privacy Policy for Range

**Effective date:** _13 08 2026_
**App:** Range (`com.vythera.range`)
**Developer / data controller:** _Vythera_
**Contact:** _narzo9990@gmail.com_

Range helps you work out where you can afford to travel. You give it a budget, a
date and a few preferences, and it estimates the full cost of a trip to each
destination it knows about. This policy explains what data Range accesses, why,
and who it is shared with.

## 1. Information Range accesses

**a) The trip details you enter (on-device only).**
Your budget, travel date, number of travellers, nights, transport preferences,
comfort level, starting city and currency are held on your device so the app can
price trips and remember your choices between sessions. They are **never**
transmitted anywhere.

**b) Trips and destinations you save (on-device only).**
Saved trips and your wishlist are stored locally using Android's DataStore. They
are not backed up to any server operated by Vythera.

**c) Exchange rates (a request to a third party).**
To keep prices accurate, Range fetches current exchange rates from a public,
keyless endpoint. This request contains **no personal data** — no account, no
device identifier, no trip details, no location. It asks only for the current
value of a fixed list of currencies against the US dollar. If the request fails
or you are offline, Range uses rates shipped inside the app and carries on
working normally.

## 2. Information Range does NOT collect

- No account or sign-in; Range has no user accounts.
- No name, email, phone number, or contacts.
- No location of any kind — the "starting city" is a city you pick from a list,
  not a device location reading.
- No photos, files, microphone, or camera access.
- **No analytics and no crash-reporting SDK.** Range does not measure how you use
  it.
- No advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no data is sold or shared for
  advertising.

## 3. Third-party services

- **Frankfurter** (<https://www.frankfurter.app/>) — public exchange-rate data,
  published from European Central Bank reference rates. Range sends only a list
  of currency codes. No key, no account, no personal data.

Range uses no other third-party service, SDK, or backend.

## 4. Permissions Range requests

- `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` — solely for the exchange-rate refresh
  described above.
- `VIBRATE` — haptic feedback on the budget ruler and controls.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

All of your data lives on your device. Clearing the app's storage, or
uninstalling Range, removes every saved trip, wishlist entry and setting
permanently. There is nothing stored on a server to request deletion of.

## 6. Children

Range is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from
anyone.

## 7. Accuracy of prices

Range's trip totals are **estimates produced by a model that runs on your
device** — built from distance, seasonality, booking lead time, local cost of
living and your stated preferences. They are not quotes, not live fares, and not
an offer to sell travel. Always confirm real prices with the airline, operator or
accommodation before booking.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the revised
policy published in this repository.
